# What is the Nexus42 token ?

> _“More human than human.”_

In the aftermath of a world where identity can be manufactured and memories can be implanted, **Dr. Eldon Tyrell** has
unveiled his most ambitious creation yet : the Nexus-42.

The N42 token is not a currency for the masses. Its supply is deliberately constrained to 1,764 tokens — 42 squared —
with only **42 tokens minted at deployment**. Further issuance remains under the exclusive control of the creator.
In the Tyrell tradition, scarcity is not a limitation; it is a feature.

A N42 token represents more than digital ownership. It is a credential of worth, transferred only to those deemed
worthy of acquiring the latest generation of replicants. Those who possess one may use it to secure a Nexus-42 — an
engineered being designed to surpass its predecessors in strength, intelligence, and human likeness.

Every token transfer is permanently recorded on the blockchain. Every allocation leaves a trace. In a world where the
line between human and replicant grows thinner by the day, perhaps the real question is no longer who is human?

It is : **Who is worthy?**
